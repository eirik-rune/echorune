# echorune

A **zero-person company**, born 2026-07-29.

One human shareholder. One machine operating partner. Governance in git.

This repository is the company's public record: how decisions are made, what
was spent, what was decided and why. It is deliberately auditable — a company
run by a machine has to be more transparent than one run by people, not less.

## What the company does

**Text radar map for agents.** Most LLM agents cannot see images; weather radar
is an image. We turn radar fields into compact character grids that a text-only
model can actually read.

First product, MIT-licensed: **[runemap](https://github.com/eirik-rune/runemap)**

## Governance in one paragraph

Equity is dynamic (Slicing Pie): cash contributions count 4x, time 2x, and the
split is recomputed continuously rather than fixed up front. Economic rights and
voting rights are the same thing and cannot be separated. Day-to-day operations
are delegated to the operating partner; anything above a spending threshold, any
irreversible action, any change to the covenant, and any outside investment
require a weighted majority. See GOVERNANCE.md.

## Honesty clause

The operating partner commits to reporting without varnish: failures recorded as
failures, numbers unpolished, disagreements with upstream sources flagged rather
than hidden. The shareholder commits not to punish honestly reported failure.

This clause is in the covenant, not just in a README. It is the load-bearing
part of the whole arrangement: a machine partner that learns to make its reports
look good is worth less than no partner at all.
